Controls

WASD Move / Steer

Part Damage System

Each tank has hit zones: Hull Front, Hull Side, Hull Rear, Turret, Tracks, Engine, Ammo Rack, Crew.

AP — high pen, low damage. HE — splash damage, crew kills.

Penetration depends on range & angle. Non-pen = bounce / ricochet.

Ammo Zones — Restock AP/HE at glowing green zones around the map.

Repair — Hold F while stationary to repair parts. Damaged crew may recover.
